📖 How to Use

  1. Use the left panel to search for a parcel by Plot ID or road name.
  2. Click a result and press Apply to highlight it on the map.
  3. Use the right panel to explore archive files, 360° panoramas, KROKI plans, 3D view and CCTV.
  4. Right-click the header to open Comments, this guide, or About.
  5. Use Comments to record and track notes per parcel. Data is saved locally and can be exported as JSON or TXT.

DQ Land Information System

A spatial archive and land-record management platform for the Diplomatic Quarter.

  • Archive sources: Deeds, Survey, Permits, CAD, PDF, Images
  • Integrated 360° panorama, 3D scene and CCTV viewers
  • Comments module with local JSON/TXT export
